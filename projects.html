Web app · 2024 – 2026

Hitting the Vibe

Built on Spotify playlists

Automatically selects tracks from event guests’ playlists to generate a shared playlist.

  • Method. Tracks scored by danceability, energy and acousticness; each guest’s top tracks filtered by stylistic similarity to a final 25.
  • Findings. Spotify audio features alone barely distinguish party songs (AUC 0.48–0.60); a small preprocessing change replaces 10 of 25 tracks.
  • Origin. A four-person NYU course project, later developed independently into a web app.
  • Note. Demo uses simulated guests and a public 2020 Spotify dataset (Spotify’s audio-features API is closed to new apps).

Course project · Summer 2026

Brain Tumor MRI Classification

Data Mining Methodology II, UCF · team of two

Best model: 75.9% accuracy but only 23% sensitivity for glioma; overall accuracy masked class-level differences.

  • Method. F-test selection and PCA on pixel features of 3,264 MRI scans across four classes; nine models compared, from regularized regression and tree ensembles to a CNN.

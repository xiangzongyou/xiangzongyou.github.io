2026 · audio

AI Music Detection on Unseen Generators

Generative models emerge rapidly; detectors must handle models they have never seen. This study trains detectors on four text-to-music models and holds out a fifth for testing.

2026 · symbolic

Testing a Beethoven Classifier on Recombined Imitations

AI systems can imitate a composer by splicing fragments of the composer’s own works. Using Beethoven as a case study, this study tests whether a classifier can recognize such imitations.
